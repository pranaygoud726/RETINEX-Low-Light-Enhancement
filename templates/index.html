THE TECHNOLOGY

How Retinex works

Retinex models an image as the product of illumination and reflectance.

I(x,y) = R(x,y) × L(x,y)

01 — Log transformation
Convert multiplication into addition using logarithms.

02 — Illumination estimation
Use Gaussian blur to estimate slowly changing illumination.

03 — Reflectance extraction
Subtract estimated illumination in the logarithmic domain.

04 — Image enhancement
Normalize the result and preserve natural image colours.
